Indirect auto financing is an arrangement in which a car dealership helps secure financing for a customer through a network of finance companies and funding partners. Instead of arranging financing on their own before shopping for a vehicle, customers complete the financing process at the dealership, allowing vehicle selection and financing to happen within the same transaction. This streamlined approach benefits both customers and dealerships.
