In auto financing, securing an application approval is often only the first step. Before an auto contract can be funded, the finance company may require additional documentation known as stipulations, or "stips." Missing or unclear stips can delay funding, create additional work for dealership staff, and lead to repeated and back-and-forth requests for information throughout the process.
