Agora Data Co-Founder, President, and Chief Operating Officer Matt Burke recently joined the Used Car News Podcast with host Jeffrey Bellant to discuss his new role as President of the National Automotive Finance Association, the organization's 30-year history, and key auto finance trends. The conversation covered Burke’s background in the business, how Agora approaches dealer participation in auto finance portfolios, and what he sees ahead for the industry as technology and risk dynamics continue to evolve.
