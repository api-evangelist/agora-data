When respected industry voices start talking about dealer financing challenges, dealers take notice. Recently, Car Dealership Guy featured findings from an auto dealer financing study commissioned by Agora. The study surveyed 414 independent dealers to better understand the realities of today's dealer-lender relationship and uncover the operational challenges dealers face every day.
